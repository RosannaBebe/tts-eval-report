1核心结论Key findings

采用行业通用MOS（Mean Opinion Score）测评方法，评估得出：

自研TTS具备替换当前正在使用的Elevenlabs V2.5的质量条件，Leo与Kim音色与Elevenlabs V3的质量基本持平。Max下一步优化重点为消除伪影问题。

自研TTS在三个老师音色的“韵律”表现上，明显好于当前使用的V2.5版本，即：表现得更像老师。

稳定性与伪影方面，更新后的自研Kim明显好于当前V2.5的表现，自研Leo、Max与当前V2.5表现基本持平，即：自研TTS在表达强情绪类的语句时，更少出现破音、爆音或音色突变类问题，同时，更低概率出现电流音或噪音。

准确性与完整度方面自研TTS与V2.5表现也基本持平，整体处于行业内较高MOS评分水平。

Using the industry-standard MOS (Mean Opinion Score) method, we find:

In-house TTS is ready to replace the ElevenLabs V2.5 currently in use. Leo and Kim are essentially on par with ElevenLabs V3. Max’s next priority is removing artifacts.

Across all three teacher voices, in-house TTS is clearly stronger than production V2.5 on prosody — it sounds more like a teacher.

On stability and artifacts, the updated in-house Kim is clearly better than current V2.5; in-house Leo and Max are roughly even with V2.5. That is: in strong-emotion lines, in-house TTS is less likely to crack, clip, or jump in timbre, and less likely to add hiss or noise.

Accuracy and completeness are also essentially even with V2.5, at a high MOS level for the industry.

3测评方法与评分维度Evaluation method and scoring dimensions

采用行业通用MOS测评方法，在盲评机制下，由3名不同的评委对自研或ElevenLabs TTS打分（界面不显示 ElevenLabs / 自研）后取平均分。 We use the industry-standard MOS method under a blind protocol: three different raters score in-house or ElevenLabs TTS (the UI never shows ElevenLabs / in-house), then we take the average.

维度边界定义Dimension boundaries

维度Dimension 关注以下问题：Focuses on:
准确性Accuracy错读、多读、乱读、重复读；严重含混导致听不出词Wrong, extra, garbled, or repeated words; severe slurring so a word cannot be heard
完整度Completeness漏读、句中 / 句末截断、读到一半消失Missing words, mid- or end-of-sentence cutoffs, dying out halfway
稳定性Stability破音、爆音、忽大忽小、音色突变Cracks, pops, wild loudness swings, sudden timbre jumps
韵律情感Prosody & emotion节奏、停顿、重音、语气 / 情绪是否贴文本Rhythm, pauses, stress, and whether tone / emotion match the text
伪影Artifacts底噪、电流音、咔哒声、叠音残响、环境怪声Hiss, electrical buzz, clicks, echo leftovers, ambient odd sounds
评分Rating 课堂可用性Classroom usability
1几乎不能听 / 语义毁了 · 不能用Almost unlistenable / meaning destroyed · unusable
2明显硬伤，当示范不放心 · 不建议用Clear hard defects; unsafe as a model · not recommended
3一般可用：小瑕疵，不影响跟读主信息 · 能用Usable: small flaws that do not hide the main line for shadowing · good enough
4清楚自然，仅细毛刺 · 推荐用Clear and natural, only fine nicks · recommended
5干净、准、稳、语气贴 · 示范级（宜少）Clean, accurate, stable, tone-fit · model-level (should be rare)
① 准确性 Accuracy（读的对不对）① Accuracy (is it read correctly)

维度描述：以「关键词 / 教学目标词是否读对、整句是否可懂」为主；1 处严重错读（改词、目标词错成别的）即可评低于2分。Dimension description: focus on whether key / target words are correct and the sentence is intelligible. One serious misread (changed word, target word as something else) can already be scored below 2.

分Score标准Rubric
1关键词大面积听不出；或乱读 / 反复重复；整句语义翻掉Key words largely unintelligible; or garbled / looping repeats; sentence meaning flipped
21 处及以上明显错读 / 增读 / 重复，或将目标词（如 flamingo / come on）读到影响了学生的跟读One or more clear misreads / extras / repeats, or a target word (e.g. flamingo / come on) read in a way that hurts the student’s shadowing
3偶有极轻含混或非关键词轻微含糊，大意仍对；无重复、无改词Occasional very light slurring or a non-key word slightly vague; gist still correct; no repeats or word changes
4词准；最多 1 处极轻含混且不歧义Words accurate; at most one very light, unambiguous slur
5与文本一致，吐字清楚，无错读、无增读、无重复读Matches the text; clear articulation; no wrong, extra, or repeated words
② 完整度 Completeness（有没有说完）② Completeness (did it finish the line)

维度描述：不存在「大段漏读」的情况。同时，关注词是否被读全，句末是否有被切开的情况，例如：句末只读了半个词。Dimension description: there are no long-passage omissions. Also check whether words are fully spoken and whether the ending is cut off — for example, only half of the last word is read.

分Score标准Rubric
1缺关键信息（问句没问完、指令没说）或开头 / 结尾截到句子不成立Missing key information (question unfinished, instruction not spoken) or the start / end is cut so the sentence does not stand
2明显漏词或半截词 / 半截句，仍能猜一点意思但不能当干净示范Clear missing or half words / half sentence; some gist remains but it is not a clean model
3词都在；句末略急但最后一个词仍可辨All words present; ending a bit rushed but the last word is still identifiable
4完整；最多收尾略紧，词仍完整可跟读Complete; ending may be slightly tight, but words stay whole and shadowable
5与文本一一对应，无漏读、截断，收尾完整One-to-one with the text; no omissions or cutoffs; clean finish
③ 稳定性 Stability（发声是否炸 / 飘）③ Stability (does the voice crack / drift)

维度描述：几乎没有「中间缓冲」；测试原则：一次句尾炸裂 ≈ 整条不可用。强情绪句子（Come on! / Guess! / Wow!）是本测试的重点压。Dimension description: there is almost no “middle buffer.” Test principle: one end-of-line blowout ≈ the whole clip is unusable. Strong-emotion lines (Come on! / Guess! / Wow!) are the main stress of this test.

分Score标准Rubric
1破音 / 电音贯穿，或整句发飘失控，几乎无法听完Cracking / electric timbre throughout, or the whole line drifts out of control; almost impossible to finish listening
2有明显破音 / 电音 / 爆音（含单次句尾炸裂），听感刺耳，课堂不能当示范Clear crack / electric timbre / pop (including a single end-of-line blowout); harsh; cannot be a classroom model
3偶有轻微毛刺 / 发紧，一闪而过，不持续、不刺耳Occasional light rasp / tightness that flashes past; not sustained or harsh
4整体稳；最多极短瞬时不稳Mostly stable; at most a very brief instant of instability
5全程稳定，无破音、电音、爆音、明显忽大忽小或音色乱跳Stable throughout; no cracks, electric timbre, pops, obvious loudness swings, or wild timbre jumps
④ 韵律情感 Prosody & Emotion④ Prosody & Emotion

维度描述：重点测评：(1) 停顿是否切错气口 (2) 重音是否在教学词 / 情感词上 (3) 语气是否匹配标点与场景（! / ? / 安抚句等）。Dimension description: Focus of scoring: (1) whether pauses cut the wrong breath points (2) whether stress is on teaching / emotion words (3) whether tone matches punctuation and scene (! / ? / soothing lines, etc.).

分Score标准Rubric
1语气完全反了或失控；或停顿碎成「逐词报站」，无法当老师话术Tone fully reversed or out of control; or pauses smashed into word-by-word station announcements — not teacher talk
2明显机械 / 机器人腔；或重音乱到目标词被淹没；或情绪与文本明显不符Clearly mechanical / robotic; or stress so wrong the target word is buried; or emotion clearly mismatches the text
3能听懂是老师在说；偶有略平、略冲或不自然小停顿，仍可用Still sounds like a teacher; occasional flatness, slight push, or unnatural small pauses; still usable
4节奏与语气较自然，贴合短句场景；仅细微不自然Rhythm and tone fairly natural and scene-fit; only tiny unnatural bits
5短句也清楚：气口对、重音对、情绪贴合（激动收尾有力但不炸——炸归稳定性）Clear even on short lines: breath, stress, and emotion fit (excited endings are strong but not blown — blowouts belong to Stability)
⑤ 伪影 Artifacts（有没有底噪怪声）⑤ Artifacts (is there hiss or odd noise)

维度描述：重点评估是否存在杂音、句首 / 句尾咔哒音、是否出现一声爆噪音、是否存在持续的电流底噪。合成前后的正常短静音、极轻房间感且不抢词的情况不算为伪影。Dimension description: Focus on whether there is noise, start/end clicks, a single burst, or a constant electrical floor. Normal short silence around synthesis, or a very light room tone that does not steal words, does not count as an artifact.

分Score标准Rubric
1强底噪 / 持续怪声 / 明显咔哒叠音，内容被盖住Strong hiss / constant odd noise / clear click-echo; content is covered
2明显底噪或间歇怪声，烦人，影响当示范听Clear hiss or intermittent odd noise; annoying; hurts use as a model
3轻微底噪或偶发轻杂音，不抢词Light hiss or occasional soft noise that does not steal words
4基本干净；需细听才察觉极轻底噪Mostly clean; only a very light floor if you listen hard
5干净，无明显底噪、电流底、咔哒、残响或其他外加怪声Clean; no obvious hiss, electrical floor, clicks, leftover echo, or other added odd sounds
